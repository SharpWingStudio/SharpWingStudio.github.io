First title

Centroid

A 2013 Xbox Live Indie Game, rebuilt. Thirty levels. Four biomes. Find a gun. Chunk some grubs.

Side-scrolling platformer-shooter. Woods, city night, mountains in the rain, then the crater. Play it alone, or with up to four on one machine.

Solo

One campaign. Woods, then the city at night, then the mountains, then the crater. Two acts, one game.

You carry the gun, the city flashlight, and the score. The run ends when you fall and there is no checkpoint.

On the couch

The same thirty levels, on one machine, with one to four players. Extra pads join in the lobby and can drop out before you start. The screen divides with the party. Each seat is a player, not a tag-along.

Two players. Each half is a viewport.

Four players. One score, one save.

In a mini-boss pit the chain guns are shared: one rider each, and anyone still outside is brought in before the fight starts. On city night every living seat has a flashlight. A seat can die and come back beside someone still up. The run ends only when the last living seat falls.

This is the couch. There is no online play.
