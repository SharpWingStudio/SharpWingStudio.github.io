First title

Centroid

A 2013 Xbox Live Indie Game, rebuilt. Thirty levels. Four biomes. Find a gun. Chunk some grubs.

Side-scrolling platformer-shooter. Woods, city night, mountains in the rain, then the crater. Solo or local split-screen — same campaign, up to four on the couch.

The campaign

A peek at each biome. The rest of the thirty stays in the game.

Couch co-op

New campaign opens a lobby. Extra pads sit down. You run the same 30-level war in local split-screen — one to four players, no online required.

The war

A shard of Centra hits Earth. Centroids pour out. You stop the source — then they come back harder, through the mountains and into the crater.

One game. Two acts. No sequel SKU.
