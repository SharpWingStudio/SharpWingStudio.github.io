First title · Coming to Steam

Centroid

A fragment of shattered Centra hits Earth and feral Centroids pour out. One soldier stops the source — then the worms return worse than ever.

Development captures from the woods, city night, and crater campaign. Art is still being rebuilt for Steam.

Act I — First impact

Long ago, peaceful wormlike creatures called Centroids lived on Centra. An asteroid shattered their homeworld. Survivors burrowed as the planet broke apart. Scattered fragments drifted. Vegetation died. Hunger and asteroid minerals turned them feral.

A shard of Centra hits Earth. Centroids pour out of the meteor. War begins.

Find a gun. Chunk some grubs. Stop the source.

Act II — They came back

One soldier from the Midwest reached the crash site and stopped the source. The Centroids were believed eliminated.

They came back. There are more than ever.

Double up on guns. Chunk some grubs. Both chapters ship as one game — not a sequel SKU, not a remake that erases the original. The war you thought was over is the first half of the campaign.

The game

  • Genre Side-scrolling platformer-shooter
  • Fantasy Move, aim independently, and clear Centroids with an escalating arsenal
  • Campaign Dual-act solo run — original Earthfall war, then the return
