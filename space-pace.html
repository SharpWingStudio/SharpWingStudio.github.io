Space Pace

A chase-view cockpit space shooter. Pick a ship, fly the lane, out-punch a rogue AI who has turned to selling to the bad guys.

Sharpwing, Needle, and Bulwark. Ten sectors, a seven-lesson training lane, and a capital mothership you have to beat to the beam. Drawn on Peregrine.

Chase view 3 ownships 10 sectors Solo

The three ownships

Three ownships with real tradeoffs. The one beside the menu is the live ship model.

Tradeoffs

ShipCharacterUnlock
SharpwingSlender, dart-like. The studio craft.Available from the start
NeedleExtremely thin, needle-like. The most elongated of the three.2 clears
BulwarkShort, fuller, rounded armored craft. The stubbiest of the three.5 clears

Unlocks are earned by clearing sectors. The ship you pick is the one you fly.

Seated

Steer, aim, laser, missile, boost, brake, barrel roll, and warp. The seat is one continuous loop, and the cockpit LCD samples the live color target.

Chase

The outside view is the chase. The reticle charges, locks, and fades during a warp. Boost exhaust is the particle plume on the aft bells.

The lane

New Game picks a slot, a pilot name, and a ship. If training is not done, the ship step offers a seven-lesson lane. Clearing it sets your profile and opens sector 1. Sectors 1 through 9 warp into the next.

The clear

A Level Clear beacon is a ring and a glowing core. Collect it and the run clears, adding the bonus plus the shield you keep. Shield at zero plays the death cam, then FAILURE with RETRY and MAIN MENU. A higher run updates the Best score.

Rigma

Sector 10 is Rigma — a rogue sentient AI mothership who sells to the mercenary and bandit colonies. She is the end of the campaign and the one fight you have to win by out-flying the capital beam.

Rogue AI Capital beam Sector 10

Sector 10 is the capital fight: hex plates, a circuitry shell, a core door, and a beam you have to slip.
